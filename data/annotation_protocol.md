# Annotation Protocol

Dataset: Indian Classical Instrument Music Emotion Dataset

Instrument Classes:
- Flute
- Tabla
- Veena
- Violin

Emotion Classes:
- Calm
- Happy
- Relax
- Sad

Each audio clip was assigned two labels:

1. Instrument label
2. Emotion label

The dataset was organized according to instrument-emotion categories and used for supervised dual-task learning.

Dataset Split:

Training Set: 539 clips
Validation Set: 77 clips
Testing Set: 154 clips

All experiments were performed using a fixed train-validation-test split to ensure reproducibility.
